Rift sounds from Freesound, made mono, run through a glitch (slices stuttered, reversed, pitch-jumped or cut out, then
bitcrushed and downsampled; _og is the setting picked, _s<seed> the same setting with another seed) and brought to
matched loudness. The loops have their last moments crossfaded into their start.
